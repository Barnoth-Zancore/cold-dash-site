Privacy policy

Cold Dash for Android, iOS and the web · Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

Cold Dash has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or any other personal details. Your progress stays on your device. The free apps show optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

What the game stores on your device

Your scores, fish, unlocked characters, hats and trails, missions, daily streak and settings (sound, vibration, touch hints) are saved in the game's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Ads (Android and iOS apps)

When a run ends you can choose to watch a short video ad to keep going. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with the ad. Google is responsible for this processing; see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • iPhone and iPad: Apple asks whether Cold Dash may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • Watching ads is always optional; you can continue a run with fish instead, or not at all.

The web version

The browser version stores your progress in your browser's local storage and works offline. It does not show ads unless stated on the page.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, contact lists, location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No in-game chat or sharing of personal information.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't receive personal data from Google AdMob.

Children

Cold Dash is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
